MoodSync Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 · Version 1.0
Summary: MoodSync is a single-purpose Chrome Extension that translates natural language mood descriptions into Spotify playlists. We do not sell your data, track your browsing history, inject advertisements, or access personal messages. All credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

1. Information We Collect and Receive

When you use MoodSync, we access only the minimal information required to provide playlist generation:

  • Spotify Profile Information: Your Spotify display name, user ID, and subscription tier (Free vs. Premium). Used to identify your account and create playlists on your behalf.
  • Natural Language Mood Text: The text descriptions you type into the extension (e.g. "rainy evening lofi"). This is sent to our backend to generate musical anchors.
  • Spotify OAuth Tokens: Short-lived access tokens and refresh tokens received during OAuth consent. These are AES-256 encrypted on our server and never exposed in browser extension code.
  • Playback State: If you use the "Queue Next" button, MoodSync checks if you have an active Spotify player device.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do not collect or monitor your web browsing history, active tabs, or bookmarks.
  • ❌ We do not collect your password (authentication occurs directly on Spotify's official servers).
  • ❌ We do not sell or monetize personal data in any form.
  • ❌ We do not use third-party tracking scripts or advertising SDKs.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To generate search anchors (genres, artists, eras) corresponding to your mood.
  • To search the official Spotify Web API and curate matching tracks.
  • To create playlists on your authorized Spotify account.
  • To queue tracks on your active Spotify playback device when requested.

4. Data Storage and Security

Spotify access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. The browser extension only receives an opaque, short-lived session token stored in browser session storage. Sessions automatically expire after 24 hours of inactivity.

5. Third-Party Services

MoodSync interacts with the following services:

  • Spotify Web API: For account authentication, track searching, and playlist creation under Spotify's Developer Terms.
  • AI / LLM Providers: Mood text is processed to extract genres and artists without including personal user identifiers.

6. User Control and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Revoke Access: You can revoke MoodSync's permissions at any time via your Spotify Apps Dashboard.
  • Log Out: Clicking the logout icon in the extension immediately clears your session from the browser and server.
  • Data Deletion Request: To request deletion of your account record and stored requests, contact us at support@moodsync.app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated effective date.
